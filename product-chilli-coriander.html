Red Chilli & Coriander

Two of India's most widely traded spices, sourced from established growing regions and prepared for export in whole and processed forms.

Red Chilli

Whole dried red chillies sourced from leading chilli-growing belts of India, offered in a range of varieties to suit different heat levels, colour value and end uses — from whole pod export to processing into powder or flakes.

OriginAndhra Pradesh, Karnataka & other growing regions, India
VarietyWhole dried pods (Capsicum annuum); multiple varieties available
Quality GradeStemless/with stem, sorted by size and colour on request
MoistureTypically below 12%
Packing Options25 kg / 50 kg PP bags; custom export packing on request

Coriander

Coriander seeds sourced primarily from Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh and Gujarat. Available whole or split, cleaned and sorted for export, and suitable for whole-spice trading, grinding, or use as a raw material in spice blends.

OriginRajasthan, Madhya Pradesh & Gujarat, India
VarietyWhole and split coriander seed (Coriandrum sativum)
PurityTypically 98%+, subject to lot
MoistureTypically below 10%
Packing Options25 kg / 50 kg PP bags; custom export packing on request

Specifications vary according to crop season, buyer requirement and available lot. Exact specifications, including pungency/SHU level for chilli, are confirmed with the buyer before shipment and are not guaranteed to be identical across every consignment.
