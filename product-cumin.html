Cumin Seeds

Cumin seeds sourced from Gujarat and Rajasthan, two of India's leading cumin-growing regions. Machine-cleaned and sorted to remove foreign matter, and available in a range of purity levels to suit food processing and retail packing requirements.

Typical Specifications

OriginGujarat & Rajasthan, India
VarietyWhole cumin seed (Cuminum cyminum), single & double cleaned options
PurityTypically 98% – 99.9% (Europe/Sortex-clean grade on request)
MoistureTypically below 8%
CleaningMachine cleaned; Sortex (color-sorted) cleaning available on request
Shelf LifeUp to 18 months when stored in a cool, dry place
Packing Options25 kg / 50 kg PP bags; buyer-specific bag size, material and labeling on request

Specifications vary according to crop season, buyer requirement and available lot. Exact specifications are confirmed with the buyer before shipment and are not guaranteed to be identical across every consignment.
