Quality & Packaging

How we prepare every shipment for export, and the packaging options available to buyers.

Quality Checks

Incoming lots are checked for moisture, foreign matter and visual quality before being approved for export. Samples can be shared with buyers prior to shipment on request.

Quality Assurance & Testing

Every batch is tested against our internal quality parameters to confirm it meets the desired quality standard before it is approved for export.

Cleaning & Processing

Depending on the product, we offer machine cleaning, sorting and grading to match the purity and grade requested by the buyer.

Export Documentation

We prepare the commercial documentation required for export shipments and coordinate with buyers on Incoterms and shipping requirements.

Packaging Options

  • ✓ Standard export bags: 25 kg & 50 kg PP or jute bags
  • ✓ Custom bag sizes and materials on request
  • ✓ Container loading (LCL & FCL) as per order quantity
  • ✓ Custom labeling and private branding available on request

Available packaging, labeling and certification options may vary by product and order size. Please confirm exact requirements with our team when placing an enquiry.
